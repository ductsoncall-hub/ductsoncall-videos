About the channel

The archive of a working duct-cleaning crew.

Ducts On Call is a licensed, insured, NADCA-certified air duct and dryer vent cleaning company serving Madison and the surrounding area. The channel started in 2018 with raw footage from real jobs and grew into a library of homeowner guides and short, source-cited explainers about the things that affect the air inside your home: mold, dust, humidity, radon, carbon monoxide, filtration and more.

Every video here links back to YouTube. Search by what you are worried about, filter by topic, or browse the oldest clips to see the work up close.
